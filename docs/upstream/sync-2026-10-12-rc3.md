## 1. What arrives, by subject

### 1a. Data tracks are ripped — `7e9549e`, `70507aa`, `4f5ccd9`, `19cad95`

A data track, which the CUE sheet already named as a `BINARY` file nothing
wrote, is now copied out sector by sector: MMC READ CD with every field but
the sub-channel, falling back to libcdio's raw reads on an image. Bad sectors
are retried `-r` times, then zeroed and counted as an error. Every sector's
EDC is checked and a mismatch retried like a failed read. The whole track is
CRC32'd. `-X` (`--no-data-tracks`) skips them. The CUE sheet gives a mode 2
track as `MODE2/2352`.

**Measured, not read from the diff**: upstream's own binary on its own
`mixed.cue` leaves `1.bin` (352,800 bytes) beside `2.flac` and `3.flac`, and
logs `Track 1 is data:`, `Track 1 ripped successfully!`, `CRC32:` and
`Sector EDC:    all 150 sectors match`.

This is the largest behavioural change in the sync, and it is on by default.

### 1b. The Enhanced CD session gap and the disc IDs — `6f163a4`, `289b493`, `258e81b`, `47dd70d`, `e159954`, `89c67de`

- **`6f163a4` fixes lost audio**: a trailing data track was taken for a second
  session unconditionally, so on a single-session mixed mode CD the last audio
  track lost 11,400 frames (upstream measured 152 s short on a real disc). The
  gap is now assumed only when the drive gave no pregap and did not report the
  disc single-session.
- **This collides with ours.** Our `CDEXTRA_SESSION_GAP` path refuses a gap
  that does not fit and logs it, and `(less %i frame CD-Extra session gap,
  read to: %i)` reports a cut that happened. The merge keeps **both**:
  upstream's condition for whether to cut, and our guard and log line for
  whether the cut fits and what it removed.
- The disc IDs now follow libdiscid: the ID lead-out stands at the track after
  the last audio one, the TOC is indexed by track number, and a degenerate TOC
  skips ID generation instead of indexing `tracks[-1]`. AccurateRip's request
  is fixed for discs with data tracks, and its per-track entries skip them.
  `AccurateRip ID:` is logged.
- **Who it changes**: only discs with data tracks or not starting at track 1.
  For every other disc, every ID is unchanged — the golden reference's disc
  has no data track, and §7 will say whether its IDs moved.

### 1c. AcoustID — `c78e519`, `c1743ee`, `44ebc0f`, `6e1a349`, `746f5ea`

With libchromaprint, every audio track is fingerprinted while it is read, over
its first two minutes. At the end, if every track is accurately ripped
according to AccurateRip and matched to a MusicBrainz recording, the
fingerprints are **submitted to AcoustID — only with the user's own key**,
given via `-k` upstream. Otherwise they are written to the files' tags as
`ACOUSTID_FINGERPRINT`. `-B` (`--no-acoustid`) turns fingerprinting off. Files
are written after the whole disc whenever fingerprinting is on, as they
already are with ReplayGain on, which is the default.

- **For Platterpus specifically**: it passes `-N`, so no recording is ever
  matched and **nothing is ever submitted**. A build with libchromaprint would
  write the fingerprint tag to every file; a build without it logs
  `AcoustID:       unsupported (built without libchromaprint)`.
- **It collides with our `-k`**, §2.
- **It collides with our `-Z` spool**: upstream restarts the fingerprint on
  every pass and keeps the last pass's; this fork encodes one spooled read,
  the one the reads agreed on, which need not be the last. The fingerprint
  must be taken from the read that is kept, or the tag describes audio that is
  not in the file. §6 says how.

### 1d. Pregaps and the CUE sheet — `a95bc41`, `4b0040f`, `bbf444c`, `bc3002e`

The signature LSNs are taken after the pregap handling instead of from the
raw TOC; a disc starting at a later track finds the previous track by
position; a hidden track split off as track 0 appears as track 1's `INDEX 00`;
a quote in a CUE value no longer ends its string. All four touch code this
fork rewrote (`pregap.c`, `cue_writer.c`, the `Pregap LSN:` block). **`a95bc41`
changes what `Start LSN:`/`End LSN:` report for merged and split pregaps**,
which Platterpus's LSN arithmetic reads; §7 will state, from the golden
reference and the pregap scenarios, whether any value on a default rip moved.

### 1e. Drive behaviour — `fc99e48`, `0be45a3`, `59b3f2d`

- An all-zero MCN or ISRC is treated as absent.
- **`-S` is tried even when the drive does not advertise speed selection**,
  and given up only if the drive refuses; upstream measured an iHAS324 Y that
  honours it without advertising it. **And upstream's `Speed:` line drops its
  `(changeable)`/`(unchangeable)`**, on the same evidence.
- Each track's subcode pre-emphasis is read after reading a sector inside the
  track, since READ SUB-CHANNEL reports wherever the drive last read.

All three are drive I/O and **need verification on real hardware**; no image
reaches them.

### 1f. Options and naming — `37af2e4`, `60a7bf3`, `2808a2c`, `f0079ef`, `b194e32`, `a9dfb79`, `a8f8d51`, `e843c0b`, `dad28a4`

genopt rejects NaN and out-of-range numbers and allows long-only options;
naming schemes are checked before ripping and refuse braces inside braces; a
Windows drive letter survives sanitising; macOS's colon is actually
sanitised. **genopt's range errors now print the argument as given**, which
moves argv refusal messages — `tools/probe-argv-surface.py --gate` and
`docs/seam-commands.md` §7 will say whether any row moved.

### 1g. MusicBrainz and tags — `bde6a1e`, `b5f5f95`

`-R` can name a release the DiscID does not lead to; the recording ID is also
tagged `MUSICBRAINZ_TRACKID`. Platterpus passes `-N`, so neither runs for it.

### 1h. Already done here — `1689e84`, `76dcd5d`

Upstream now exits non-zero when refusing to rip without an offset and when
`-f` finds none, and no longer trusts AccurateRip and cover art replies to be
well formed. **This fork did both first** (`.20`'s `-f` exit, the
`Offset is unset!` abort as `fatal_abort`, and the three AccurateRip parser
defects in `SETTLED.md`). Ours are kept; upstream's cover art half is taken.

### 1i. The logged track duration — `7ba4a3f`

Upstream fixes the same off-by-a-frame `Duration:` this fork fixed earlier,
from `nb_samples`. Ours is kept, with upstream's branch for data tracks.

### 1j. Tests, CI, docs — `7838f15`, `76220e8`, `90b057c`, `58120ab`, `3e18684`, `885d815`, `e2f3de6`, `f48011d`, `e129ad9`, `18979a9`, `b40c1f0`

Upstream's tests come in with their features. `main.yml` stays deleted here:
GitHub keys a workflow by path, and ours is `ci.yml` (`tests/workflows.py`).
`b40c1f0` skips CUE sheets starting at track 3 on libcdio 2.2.1 and later,
which renumbers them from 1: the same family of library change as the image
capability fixed here on 2026-10-12 (libcdio `998d123`).

## 3. Log text — measured

Upstream's `cyanrip_log()` formats at `f8ebf48` against `b40c1f0`, conversions
collapsed: **32 added, 4 removed.** None of the 32 is in our tree yet.

- **On every rip**: `AccurateRip ID: %_-%_-%_`; `AcoustID:       %_` or
  `AcoustID:       unsupported (built without libchromaprint)`.
- **Changed on every rip**: `Speed:          default`, where upstream had
  `default (%_)`.
- **Data tracks**: `Track %_ is data, skipping:` (under `-X`), `Track %_
  ripped successfully!`, `Track %_ ripped with errors.`, `CRC32:`, the three
  `Sector EDC:` rows, `Sector EDC mismatch at LSN %_!`, `Frame read failed at
  LSN %_!`, `Error writing to \"%_\": %_!`.
- **AcoustID**: five more, all after the rip.
- **Refusals**: `Invalid %_ naming scheme \"%_\"!`, `Invalid scheme syntax,
  \"{\" inside \"{\",`, three `Disc ... skipping` lines.
- **MusicBrainz, unreachable under `-N`**: eight.
- **Removed**: `No mediums match DiscID!`, `Release ID %_ not found in release
  list for DiscID %_!`, `Speed:          default (%_)`, `Unable to get AccuRIP
  DB data: missing CDDB ID!`. All four are in our tree, and §6 decides each.

**`Speed:` is kept as ours** in the merge: `default (changeable)` /
`default (unchangeable)` is a P2 line Platterpus reads (§4), and a P2 line is
not reworded without a round. Upstream's evidence goes on record instead: the
word `unchangeable` claims more than the capability bit establishes, since a
drive that does not advertise speed selection may still honour it. **The
rename is proposed for the next round**, with `-S`'s new behaviour beside it.

## 4. What a consumer would notice — read from Platterpus's code

Read at `platterpus@2a4abc8:src/platterpus/parsers/cyanrip_log.py`:

- **`Speed:`** feeds `_take_speed_cap()` (lines 1765–1773), which sets
  `speed_changeable` from the words `unchangeable`/`changeable`, and their
  read-speed ladder stops sending `-S` once it is `False`
  (`read_speed_ladder.py:44`, `adapters/cyanrip_backend.py:285–291`), because
  cyanrip aborted on `-S` there. **Upstream's `-S` no longer aborts on that
  drive, and upstream's line no longer carries either word.** Keeping our line
  (§3) keeps their ladder's input unchanged; taking upstream's `-S` behaviour
  only stops a refusal they already never trigger.
- **New top-level lines** (`AccurateRip ID:`, `AcoustID:`) are not fatal to
  their parser: an unclaimed top-level line is logged at debug (lines
  1398–1412). Their completeness test over committed real logs fails on one,
  so they must allowlist both before a log carrying them is committed there.
- **A data track's block** opens on `Track %_ is data:`, which `_TRACK_START`
  already matches (line 277); `Track %_ ripped successfully!`, `CRC32:` and
  `Sector EDC:` inside it are new to them, and so is the `.bin` file it leaves.

**So the merge is handshake material with a consumer prerequisite**: their
parser should accept the two new disc lines and the data-track rows before a
release of ours carries them.

## 5. Recommendation and sequence

1. **Merge into `platterpus-fork` now.** A merge is not a release: a build of
   the tip says `NOT a released build` in every log, and Platterpus pins a SHA.
2. **Version `0.9.4-rc3+platterpus.23`**: upstream's base and our next number
   move together, as rc1+`.6` became rc2+`.7`. Nothing is released by it, and
   **HR-1.3 forbids a release, and any lap citing a commit, until HR-1.7
   passes**, so `.23` waits for that whatever else is ready.
3. **Announce in our next lap** once HR-1 allows it: §2's `-k` divergence,
   §3's lines, §4's prerequisite, the `Speed:` rename proposal, and data tracks
   on by default.
4. **Release `.23` only after** their parser accepts the new lines.

| `src/cue_writer.c` | 2 | upstream's `after_htoa`/`tm` hidden-track layout, our `cue_file_written` and `crip_deemphasis_active()` |
| `src/cyanrip_log.c` | 4 | both includes; our round-32 track-1 pregap convention plus upstream's merged-pregap end; our duration plus upstream's data-track branch; our `Speed:`, `C2 errors:` and `Encoder:` lines (§3) |

| `tests/gen_fixtures.py`, `tests/meson.build`, `tests/rip_images.py` | 7 | both sides' tests; ours registered as ours, upstream's as theirs |

`src/cyanrip_main.c`, hunk by hunk:

1. **Pregap LSN**: our `cyanrip_get_track_pregap_lsn()` with its source,
   upstream's data mode beside it.
2. **Session gap**: upstream's condition, our fit guard and log line (§1b).
3. **`search_for_drive_offset()`**: ours — 0 for success, and the stop and
   reason states the `-j` record reports.
4. **ISRC**: ours, with upstream's all-zero test as a new reason.
5. **Subcode pre-emphasis**: upstream's read-inside-the-track first, behind
   our image gate.
6. **The data-track ripper**: upstream's, inside our pipeline — the stall
   watchdog around its reads, `read` wording for its outcome only if the round
   agrees (until then upstream's `ripped`), its state in the `-j` record, and
   our interrupt accounting.
7. **Fingerprinting**: initialised beside our spool, and fed from the read
   the spool keeps (§1c).
8. **The progress line**: upstream's `crip_progress_report()`, with our
   paranoia-skip count ported into it.
9. **`Offset is unset!`**: ours, `fatal_abort` and not `total_error_count`,
   since nothing failed to read.
10. **`-f` finding nothing**: ours, the same reason.

Every resolution that changes behaviour gets a test in the same commit, or is
named in §7 as unverified.
